# Throstle & Stripes

## Platform
Web: an independent West Bromwich Albion history museum.

## Users and purpose
Marie edits and publishes the museum without web-development knowledge. Steve
maintains the implementation. Preserve researched history, sources, uncertainty
labels, credited photographs and separate competitive/wartime results.

## Confirmed constraints
Hosting and editing must remain completely free. Use existing Astro components,
GitHub content and Cloudflare hosting. The approved first editor prototype is one
copied manager page, click-to-edit followed by simple in-place typing. Nested pages
and reusable layouts are planned later. Keep the working editor intact until the
replacement passes actual editing, preview and publication acceptance.

## Brand commitments
Retain the Albion navy-and-white museum appearance, throstle identity and current
content. This work extends the editing interface, not the public site's identity.
